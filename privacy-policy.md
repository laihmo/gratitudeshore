# Privacy Policy for Gratitude Shore

Last updated: June 2026

Gratitude Shore ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## Information We Collect

Gratitude Shore is designed to help users practice gratitude and reflection.

The App may store journal entries, reflections, settings, and preferences on your device to provide its functionality.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Analytics and Technical Data

The App and its underlying platform providers may collect limited technical information such as:

- Device type
- Operating system version
- App version
- Crash reports
- Diagnostic information

This information is used solely to improve app performance, reliability, and user experience.

## Journal Entries and User Content

Your gratitude entries, diary content, and reflections are intended for your personal use.

Unless explicitly stated within the App, journal content is not shared publicly.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

## Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party service providers, including:

- Apple App Store (distribution)

These providers have their own privacy policies governing their services.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
